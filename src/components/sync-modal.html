How to Setup

  1. Open StandupNOW app on your Android phone
  2. Start the sync server (it will show IP and port)
  3. Make sure your phone and computer are on the same WiFi network
  4. Enter the IP address and port shown on your phone
  5. Click "Test Connection" to verify
  6. Click "Save & Enable Sync" to start syncing
