🕐 Timeline Feature - StandupNOW

Feature Overview

A subtle timeline connector now appears between entries, showing how much time has passed since the last entry.

  • Displays time gaps from 0min to 1d2h (26 hours max)
  • Smart formatting: 5m, 1h30m, 3h45m, 1d2h
  • Subtle design with thin vertical line and gray time badge
  • Only shows when time gap is within the specified range
